Event basis

The test follows the documented Christmas Night 2023 QLCS/bow-echo corridor from the Ripley area through Jimboomba/Tamborine toward the northern Gold Coast between about 8:20 and 8:45 pm AEST.
OBSERVED ANCHORS: Gold Coast detailed warning 7:49 pm; Very Dangerous Thunderstorm 7:56 pm; destructive-wind warning escalation 8:40 pm; Gold Coast Seaway maximum gust 106 km/h at 9:12 pm. Bureau reporting also describes a roughly 3–4 km wide, 30–50 km long damaging-to-locally- destructive wind corridor across the Gold Coast and Scenic Rim.
SIMULATED INPUTS: exact per-frame centroids, reflectivity categories, footprint areas and Doppler radial-velocity classes. These are chosen to reproduce a fast-moving intensifying severe convective line while remaining within StormTracker's existing tracking contract. They are not represented as archived Bureau radar pixels.
