# V9.15.0 draft — QFD technical-rescue source and classification gate

Date: 8 October 2026. Branch: `feature/v9.15.0-qfd-technical-rescue`.

## Why six requested QFD job types cannot yet be mapped as exact live jobs

The publicly released QFD ESCAD Current Incidents feature layer exposes
`GroupedType`, `Master_Incident_Number`, response/update timestamps,
general locality, status, attendance and **general-area** map geometry.
`GroupedType = 'RESCUE TECHNICAL'` is a category, **not** a published
swift-water or vertical subtype. It does not publish a `JobType` field.

The specific job-type labels provided for future mapping are:
- `RESCUE WATER ALL TYPES` — water rescue
- `XE RESCUE WATER` — water rescue (separate response classification)
- `RESCUE VERTICAL` — vertical rescue
- `RESCUE MOUNTAIN RESCUE` — **included**, separate from vertical rescue; relevant to SES coordination
- `RESCUE RTC LARGE MULTI` — **included**, major road crash/possible traffic impact;
  does not establish a road closure without an official road-status feed
- `ASSIST EXTREME WEATHER` — **included**, weather assistance; may be relevant
  to SES even where the reported job is not technically a rescue

A strict `classifyQfdRescueJobType` mapper is committed with unit tests.
No QFD public incident is upgraded to one of these six job types unless a
legitimate public classification field **or an official publicly released
incident title** with the matching exact label is verified for that
specific incident. These names were supplied as **internal job-type labels**;
do not treat the internal system as a public data source and never
retrieve/reproduce restricted dispatch, CAD, pager or caller details.

The current ESCAD public schema has `GroupedType`, `Location` and
`Locality`, but no `Title` or `JobType` field. A public general title
*could* be useful in another official source, but mere field presence,
free-text resemblance, location or weather conditions are insufficient
to claim a specific classification. Match incident identity and time,
check publication rights and review example records first.

Generic grouped technical rescues remain separately labelled
**subtype unspecified**. Live subtype layers remain blocked pending
verified public data. A `RESCUE WATER ALL TYPES` job is not automatically
a *swift-water* rescue: describe it as water rescue unless QFD specifies
fast-flowing-water conditions.

## Candidate development

- Optional, off-by-default general QFD technical-rescue map layer with
  publisher's general-area locations and clickable official source.
- Browser-fetch only; no new paid subscription or user-managed server.
- 10-minute polling and 30-minute source expiry/marker removal.
- No synthetic jobs, geocoded private addresses, restricted dispatch/CAD access,
  or conversion from rain, flood gauges, weather warnings or radar.
- All six classifications are within scope if genuinely present in an
  official public record; keep individual operational categories distinct.
- Mountain jobs must not be described as confirmed SES attendance solely
  because SES commonly assists. A major multi RTC is not a confirmed closure.
- Separate map cards and click/pointer handling for existing road, power
  and river-gauge panels.
- Core radar, Doppler and inferred tracking untouched.

Sources:
- Public QFD Dashboard: https://www.fire.qld.gov.au/Incident-Dashboard
- ESCAD schema: https://services1.arcgis.com/vkTwD8kHw2woKBqV/arcgis/rest/services/ESCAD_Current_Incidents_Public/FeatureServer/0?f=pjson

## Release gates

1. Validate the public query actually returns QFD data from a GitHub Pages
   browser with permitted CORS. The parser/tests alone do not prove this.
2. Verify mobile and desktop markers and official source panels.
3. Simulate feed failures, expiry, incident deletion, duplicate IDs and
   source changes. Ensure no false rescue subtype labels, invented road
   closures or inferred SES deployments appear.
4. Inspect any future public title field and confirm exact label, publication,
   incident identity and timestamp before promoting subtype markers.
5. Regress existing radar, gauges, road closures and outages.
6. Keep V9.14.0 acceptance and release milestones separate. Do **not**
   merge this draft or promote generic technical rescues as water/vertical.

Observed lightning remains the final blocked wishlist item.
