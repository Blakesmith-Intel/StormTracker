# StormTracker — rainfall reports (quick reference)

**New read-only reports | Operator information sheet | 22 October 2026**

**Where to find them:** Layers → Significant rain. Choose **Since 9 am**, **Last hour**, or **24hrs to 9am** first; both reports follow this selection.

| Report | Open from | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| **Rainfall totals** | **View rainfall totals report** directly below the rainfall layer controls | All valid stations reporting **100+ mm**, then all reporting **50–99.9 mm**, with station totals and counts. Includes *Less rare* and *Unrated* stations because this report measures **millimetres, not rarity**. |
| **Notable+ locations** | Expand **Rainfall colours and local rarity** → **View Notable+ locations report** | Every 50+ mm station meeting an official local design benchmark, grouped **Exceptional (1% AEP) → Very rare (2%) → Rare (5%) → Unusual (10%) → Notable (20%)**. *Less rare* and *Unrated* are excluded from this verified-rarity list and indicated separately. |

## Reading the reports

Each row shows **BoM station name, measured rainfall in mm, station number, local rarity, source issue time and verified catchment where available**. The window can scroll and works on mobile. Use **↻ Refresh** for the latest available BoM report and **Close** to return to the map.

**Important:** The reports reflect the selected period and all eligible stations, **not just the labels visible at the current map zoom**. Station coordinates and source IDs never change. On the map, the coloured raindrop (measured mm) and its attached AEP rarity label remain **one marker at the original BoM gauge location**.

## Interpreting rarity

AEP is the chance in any year of rainfall meeting or exceeding a local design rainfall depth **for that location and duration**. A **1% AEP** value is approximately a **1-in-100 annual chance** — *not* a once-every-100-years schedule and **not a flood classification**. A station marked **Unrated** has no verified local comparison at present, not an all-clear.

**Source and caution:** Official BoM Queensland station maps and BoM 2016 IFD rainfall-frequency references. Observations may be automatically reported with limited quality control and source update delays. StormTracker is for situational awareness; use official BoM warnings for emergency decisions.
