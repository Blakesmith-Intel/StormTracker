# StormTracker V9.18.0 — User guide

Open **https://blakesmith-intel.github.io/StormTracker/** in your desktop or mobile browser. No local installation is required.

### Radar and Doppler

Select your primary site using the radar menu or tap the dish icon on the map. The original 19 Queensland sites plus NSW Brewarrina, Moree and Grafton are available. The Radars control supports up to **three additional** sites as extra map windows. The primary site still drives storm tracks, threat cones, 3-D inference and Doppler where genuine BoM source data are supported. Reflectivity and wind frames are not fabricated to make up for mismatched timestamps. Switching to Doppler clears old rain-cell tracks and selected threat cones.

### Basemap, terrain and geographic labels

Choose **Street** or **Satellite · DEA Regional + Local Detail**. Satellite opens a nationwide historical DEA colour image designed for viewing weather systems several kilometres across, and automatically changes to official high-detail Queensland/NSW imagery when zoomed close enough to make out smaller geographic features. Going back out switches to consistent regional DEA again. There is no separate satellite-source toggle. Imagery colour or time seams may be visible at local zoom. DEA's background composite is from **2025**, not a live weather layer.

3-D terrain, map pan, tilt, rotation and zoom work independently of this satellite source. Place names are positioned **above the radar and volumetric layers**, with the same typography, decluttering and zoom behaviour across the border. Rural NSW names appear as you zoom towards the area covered by Brewarrina, Moree, Grafton and southern QLD radars; only names that fit the screen are shown, not the full available catalogue simultaneously.

### If NSW names seem absent

Wait for the short same-origin location catalogue to load, then zoom towards the selected radar coverage. On narrower mobile screens, fewer names appear so they remain legible; as you zoom in more localities become eligible. The NSW source lacks geocoded village points and a separate Grafton town point, so those omissions are intentional rather than made-up positions.

### Interpretation

StormTracker is a **situational awareness platform, not an emergency-management decision-making tool**. Compare operational information against authoritative current BoM alerts and agencies before any decisions. Government basemap and town names are reference information; the time-stamped BoM feeds supply the changing storm observations.
