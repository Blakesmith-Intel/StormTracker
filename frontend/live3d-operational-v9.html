Inference controls

3-D points and measured footprint projections appear only at ≥40 dBZ. The original BoM 2-D radar surface is unchanged.

Vertical intensity uses an empirical five-event AURA model. 1.0× is neutral; taller intense storms are presentation only, not measured cloud tops. Inferred geometry never creates or preserves track identity.
